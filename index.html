Building Open Bell ↗

A personal stock research app that brings company fundamentals, valuation, and price history together. Screen for investing ideas, keep a watchlist, explore individual companies, and see how screen results change across saved market snapshots.

The build: TypeScript and React for the interface, Neon Postgres for market snapshots, and Cloudflare Workers and Queues for background data refreshes. Yahoo Finance supplies the market data.
