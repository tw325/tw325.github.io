What I’m building

Open Bell

A clearer starting point for stock research.

Explore the app ↗

Open Bell brings company fundamentals, valuation, and price history into one place. Start with an investing idea, narrow the field, and dig into the companies that match.

The live app currently displays the name “Closing Bell.”

Find companies

Explore preset screens or adjust growth, profitability, valuation, and drawdown filters. Keep a personal watchlist for companies worth following.

Look closer

Open a company’s research page for financials and price charts, or replay a screen across saved snapshots to see how its results changed.

Behind the scenes

I built the interface and data pipeline, with saved market snapshots in Postgres and queued background refreshes. The app makes stale or missing data visible.

A little more about the build

TypeScript and React power the interface. Neon stores the market snapshots, while Cloudflare Workers and Queues coordinate ingestion. The screener reads saved data; individual company pages fetch additional Yahoo Finance data on demand.

This is a personal research prototype. Historical replay covers the retained snapshot window rather than a full historical backtest.
